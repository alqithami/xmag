# MDPI Round-1 Figure Results

This document records the per-flow figures added in response to Reviewer 2 and the exact evidence they support.

## Generated figures

The revised manuscript includes:

- `manuscript/figures/per_family_5g.pdf`: five-seed family-level AUROC and split-conformal 5% recall with one-standard-deviation error bars;
- `manuscript/figures/pareto_auroc.pdf`: communication payload versus mean unknown-family AUROC over forty matched trials;
- `manuscript/figures/roc_udpflood.pdf` and `roc_slowratedos.pdf`: five-seed ROC curves with one-standard-deviation bands;
- `manuscript/figures/score_histogram_udpflood.pdf` and `score_histogram_slowratedos.pdf`: known-versus-held-out score distributions;
- `manuscript/figures/accepted_unknown_assignments.pdf`: aggregate known-class assignments for held-out decisions that were not rejected.

## Hard-family evidence

Using retained per-flow score arrays from the detection-equivalent 24-byte float32 reference:

| Held-out family | Mean AUROC | SD | Mean recall at validation q95 | SD | Mean known FPR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| UDPFlood | 0.453255 | 0.014609 | 0.003173 | 0.004351 | 0.049618 |
| SlowrateDoS | 0.899390 | 0.018468 | 0.303479 | 0.116151 | 0.049962 |

The assignment analysis aggregates repeated decisions over five seeds, not unique flows:

- all 2,279,445 missed UDPFlood decisions were assigned to `Benign`;
- of 254,662 missed SlowrateDoS decisions, 253,875 (99.691%) were assigned to `HTTPFlood` and 787 (0.309%) to `Benign`.

These results support two distinct interpretations. SlowrateDoS is primarily absorbed into a semantically related application-layer flood class. UDPFlood instead overlaps the low-score known region and appears benign to the compact known-class head; increasing message size or changing the composite weights did not repair this failure.

## Reproduction

From a workspace containing the completed matched runs:

```bash

```

The plotting script consumes committed summary tables and the hard-holdout support generated by `export_mdpi_hard_holdout_support.py`; it does not retrain models unless a required per-flow array is missing.
